About Myself



My name is Gonasa Brian.


I was born in Khamoto Parish, Bungokho Central, Mbale District, Eastern Uganda.


I am the son of Mzee Mubala Amos. I am the second born among seven children.


My parents supported me and did their best for my education, and I thank them.


I am a Uganda Prisons Officer, a Model Farmer, an Innovator, and the Author of Khamoto.


My life is built on three things: Discipline, Hard Work, and Service to Community.

This is where I come from. This is my childhood in Khamoto.

My Education Journey


My education journey was not easy.


Primary School - Khamoto Primary School

I was just footing. I used to walk from home to Khamoto Primary School every day, on foot.


And during that village life, there was no lunch. We would go to school in the morning, learn the whole day with an empty stomach, and walk back home in the evening to eat.


It was hard. But my parents encouraged me. I did not give up.


Secondary School

When I went to town school, I started cycling. I used to cycle from Khamoto village to town school every day.


The road was bad. Rain, mud, sun, puncture. Many times I wanted to give up, but I did not give up.


Higher Education

From there I went to Oxford High School. After that I went to Busitema University and Bukalasa Agricultural College where I trained in Agriculture.


I did not get standard education easily. So I worked extra hard. I read extra. I asked questions. I learned by doing.


That journey, footing with no lunch, then cycling, taught me discipline, patience, and hard work. That is the same discipline I use today as an Officer and as a Farmer.







My Work As A Prisons Officer



I serve Uganda as a Prisons Officer.


My work teaches me discipline, order, and responsibility. I serve with respect for every person, and with commitment to reform and to community.


I believe a uniform is not for power. It is for service.

My Innovation - Innovation For Community


I believe innovation must help the village, not just the town.


I use what I learned in Agriculture to solve real problems: How to get more food from small land, how to keep animals healthy, how to save money, how to teach others.


My goal is simple: If one farmer in Khamoto succeeds, the whole village eats.







My Book - KHAMOTO


I am writing the book KHAMOTO.


Khamoto is my home. It is my story. It is the story of footing, cycling, struggling, and rising.


This book is for every child who walks to school hungry. For every parent who sacrifices. For every young person who thinks their village is too small for their dream.


Khamoto is proof that where you are born does not decide where you will end.







My Legacy - What I Want To Leave


When I am gone, I want people to say:


He served Uganda with discipline. He fed his community. He taught farmers. He inspired children from Khamoto to dream big.


That is my legacy.
