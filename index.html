My name is Gonasa Brian. I was born in Khamoto Parish, Bungokho Central, Mbale District, Eastern Uganda.

I am the son of Mzee Mubala Amos. I am the second born among seven children. My parents supported me and did their best for my education, and I thank them.

I am a Uganda Prisons Officer, an Aspiring Model Farmer, an Innovator, and the Author of Khamoto.

My life is built on three things: Discipline, Hard Work, and Service to Community.

My Education Journey

📚 Khamoto Primary School

I was just footing. I walked from home to school every day, on foot. No lunch. Morning to evening on an empty stomach. But I did not give up.

🚲 Secondary School

I started cycling from Khamoto village to town school every day. Rain, mud, sun, puncture. Many times I wanted to give up, but I did not give up.

🎓 Oxford High School

From there I went to Oxford High School and kept pushing, reading extra, asking questions, learning by doing.

🌾 Busitema & Bukalasa

Busitema University and Bukalasa Agricultural College where I trained in Agriculture. That discipline is what I use today as an Officer and as a Farmer.

My Work As A Prisons Officer

I serve Uganda as a Prisons Officer. My work teaches me discipline, order, and responsibility. I serve with respect for every person, and with commitment to reform and to community.

I believe a uniform is not for power. It is for service.

My Farming Dream — Starting Soon In Khamoto

🌱 STATUS: PREPARING TO START
I have not yet started farming. I am now in the preparation stage. This page will show my real journey, from Day 1 to first harvest.

I trained in Agriculture at Busitema University and Bukalasa Agricultural College. Now I want to put that training into practice, at home in Khamoto. I do not want to just talk about farming. I want to build a small farm that becomes a model for others.

Why I Want To Farm

Because in Khamoto, many families struggle to get enough food and income. I want to prove that with knowledge, discipline, and small land, a family can eat well and earn.

What I Want To Farm

🍫 Cocoa — For The Future

A tree crop. Plant once, earn for many years. Cocoa does well around Mt Elgon with good rain and fertile soil. Buyers, cooperatives and export market. One acre of well-managed cocoa can change a family.

🍌 Bananas — Food & Weekly Income

Every home eats bananas. Every market buys bananas. Bananas grow fast, give food and weekly income while cocoa is still growing. Good spacing, mulching and manure for big bunches.

🐟 Fish Farming — Small Space, Big Money

No big lake needed. One small pond, good water, good feeding — and you harvest fish. Fish gives protein to children and sells fast in Khamoto.

🍄 Mushrooms — Fast Money

Grows in a small house, in bags, with little land. Harvest in weeks. Hotels and markets in Mbale buy mushrooms. A good business for youth and women starting with little capital.

Cocoa for the future, Bananas for food and weekly income, Fish and Mushrooms for fast money from small space. Together, they will make a real Model Farm.

Innovation For Community

I believe innovation must help the village, not just the town. I use what I learned in Agriculture to solve real problems: how to get more food from small land, how to keep animals healthy, how to save money, how to teach others.

If one farmer in Khamoto succeeds, the whole village eats. My dream is to build a small farm that becomes a classroom for others.

My Legacy

When I am gone, I want people to say:

“He served Uganda with discipline. He fed his community. He taught farmers. He inspired children from Khamoto to dream big.”

That is my legacy.
